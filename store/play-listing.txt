-----------------------------------------------------
Built for directors. Run your production from your phone or tablet.

Mise is a complete production management suite for indie filmmakers. Call sheets, shot lists, scripts, scheduling, budgets, locations, lighting diagrams, all in one app, syncing across your crew in real time.

Why directors switch to Mise:

- Mobile-first. Works on set. Works offline. Syncs automatically when you reconnect.
- Share a project with your crew. Invite your AD, DP, producer, or PA by email. They join by signing in to Mise with the email address you invited, once that address is confirmed. Each role sees and edits only the tools it should.
- Bring what you already have. Import shot lists, crew, cast, schedules, and budgets from CSV or Excel (XLSX) spreadsheets. No retyping.
- Made for indies. Plans start at $4.99/month for one device, and grow with your crew.

What's inside:

— Call sheets with location, crew, scenes, and call times
— Shot lists with references, lighting diagrams, color notes
— Script viewer and scene-by-scene breakdown
— Scene-level shoot-day scheduling
— Crew and cast management with contact info and roles
— Locations with maps, photos, and weather
— Budget tracking with line items and categories
— Mood boards and color references
— VFX shot tracking
— Festival submission tracker
— Wrap reports and production notes
— Export to PDF, CSV, or share via your device's native share sheet

Built by an indie filmmaker. Used on real productions.

Mise was built by Simon Shih, a filmmaker and actor in Atlanta, Georgia. Every feature exists because a real production needed it. Mise is owned and operated by Page 4 Films, LLC.

Privacy-first. Your data, your call.

Sign in to sync across your devices and crew, or use Mise locally without an account. Either way, we don't sell your data, never use it to train AI, and never share it with advertisers. Read our full privacy policy at page4films.com/mise/privacy.html.

Subscription:

Plans are priced by how many devices they cover:
- Solo, 1 device: $4.99/month or $49.99/year
- Crew, up to 5 devices: $14.99/month or $149.99/year
- Production, up to 15 devices: $34.99/month or $349.99/year
- Studio, up to 40 devices: $79.99/month
- Slate, up to 250 devices: $149.99/month
Prices are in USD; Google Play shows the price in your currency before you buy.
Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled. Cancel anytime in your Google Play subscription settings.

ANDROID QA — THE PORT-CRITICAL LIST
- PDF export: VERIFIED on Android this run. Exported the budget from the app —
  expo-print -> Android WebView -> share sheet, filename slug intact, and the print
  preview is black-on-white with correct margins and an intact table.
- Table headers across page breaks (iOS issue #103): the CSS already carries
  `display: table-header-group`, which WKWebView ignores and Chromium honours.
  Rendered the 3-page call sheet through Chromium (the engine Android WebView is)
  and the cast/crew headers DO repeat on pages 2 and 3 — i.e. #103 is fixed for
  free on Android. Still worth one on-device multi-page export to close it out
  (needs a shoot day staged so the call sheet is exportable).
- expo-print, expo-sms, expo-mail-composer flows (Comms Hub) on Android
- react-native-pdf viewer renders on Android (different native lib than iOS)
- Text-size relayout at max font scale (Simon's standard)
- TABLET (Mise's best surface): verified on a 2560x1600 Pixel Tablet AVD — the tab
  bar becomes a left sidebar and the Tools grid goes 2 columns -> 3. Fixed this run:
  the sidebar's selected row drew the platform's Material BLUE fill because only the
  icon/label tint was themed; now uses Colors.accent.goldBg. Phones were never affected.
- Still unverified on tablet: landscape PDF export, split-screen/resizable windows,
  and a real 7" device (the AVD is 10.9")
